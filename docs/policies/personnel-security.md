## 1. Purpose & scope
Ensure personnel are trustworthy, trained, and granted/removed access
appropriately. Covers employees and contractors with access to Fonderie or
customer data.

## 2. Policy
### Onboarding
- **Background check** where lawful and role-appropriate, before access to
  Restricted data.
- Sign a **confidentiality/NDA** and acknowledge the Acceptable Use and
  Information Security policies.
- Access **provisioned by role, least privilege**, via an approved ticket.

### Training
- **Security awareness training at onboarding and at least annually**; completion
  is tracked.
- Engineers additionally follow the Secure SDLC practices.

### Offboarding
- On departure or role change, **all access is revoked within 24 hours**
  (**same day** for privileged/production), accounts disabled, and company assets
  returned. Coordinated with the Access Control Policy.

### Reviews
- Access is **re-certified quarterly**.

## 3. Review & enforcement
Reviewed annually. Enforcement per the Information Security Policy.
